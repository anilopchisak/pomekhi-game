# Current implementation

New rooms play **Ordinary** in [Oderzhimye with a computer Master](README.md). The organizer participates from a phone. The laptop admits five to twelve humans, hosts one room, shows the chosen prologue, gives phase calls, and displays the shared ritual. A party lasts up to seven rounds. A restored First Game party keeps its original mode, without trials.

## Prepare, deal and play

- Create a room and invite phones with the code or a LAN/Tuna link. The code in the toolbar opens the QR invitation throughout the party. The game chooses one story at room creation and saves it with the party. The organizer and shared display can read it from the Story button.
- Save the base, permitted locations, empty Spirit Dwelling, optional mission categories in Party Settings. Saving is available before players join. Preparation locks on the first deal, including after cancellation. The shared Dwelling location is used by private offering missions.
- Admission normally closes at the first deal. If fewer than five players remain before the first round, the organizer can cancel the deal and invite replacements up to five using the same room code. Roles are randomized among recipients with one extra exorcist for an odd roster. At later deals only people confirmed at the base get cards. A sole returning medium is an exorcist. Reading is untimed. The organizer sees readiness and pending replacements, can replace one card in place, and starts explicitly. Starting with unread or disconnected phones requires a warning. Pending replacements or an invalid composition block start.
- The laptop starts the placement timer, which defaults to 45 seconds. Newly possessed report a permitted location on their phone. Retained groups stay in place. A timed exorcism follows, with a default of four minutes. Exorcists explore individually. The server can end exorcism at the deadline, when a side can no longer act, or on the laptop's early call. At ordinary recall only free people return; the laptop opens untimed checking separately.
- Possessed and captured players follow the same private group card. Possessed and captured players record their own infections and can correct them individually. The exorcist who met a release condition selects participants by name. The server releases each selected group once, including every member. Group assignments, membership and reported places can survive a round boundary, and so can a group whose original holder leaves. Capturing and later releasing an exorcist never restores credit for that assignment's optional mission.

## Check and finish

Phones upload durable capture, release, mission and correction reports. A player can request review when dependencies block an undo. The organizer can resolve blocking records before approving it. Online player actions apply immediately in eligible phases. An offline mission report received after recall requires an organizer decision. The organizer can preview both decisions with their score and dependency changes. Already recorded events can upload on pause without changing the result; new gameplay reports and undo are disabled on pause. Missing group places can be confirmed during checking and carry forward without retroactively changing releases. Connected players receive automatic base or group confirmation from the accepted ledger. The organizer confirms offline returns and corrects offline infection or release in the same roster. A confirmed infection with an unknown group cancels mission credit and delays dealing until release. Connection loss alone does not establish infection or return. Unconfirmed free people miss the next deal and can be confirmed at a subsequent check. The organizer can explicitly close waiting for missing journals; closed results are immutable.

The original candle formula is N+1 / 2 / 3. Each confirmed rescue of an original assignment or eligible mission moves one candle. Players confirmed at the base can report a violation of an open prophecy on the phone during checking or while reading the next deal. The laptop reviews it and records the outcome once. Prophecies open in order and stay open after a backward step. A penalty selects the unique largest pile including candles off the board; if it is A or there is a tie, close the report without a move. The endings are victory, seventh-check defeat, confirmed total possession, no return after the configurable recall wait, and stop. A disputed release can delay the no-return decision. In all four endings everyone returns; a new party has a new room identity. Restart replaces the room in one commit while preserving the laptop catalog and used codes.

## Authored cards

The authored bank contains 75 possessed missions, 42 exorcist missions, 11 trials, 10 ordinary prophecies, the final door, six stories and two references. The organizer can enable indoor, outdoor, contact and harder missions independently. The optional comedy setting adds 24 possessed and 16 exorcist missions to the authored draw alongside the base missions. The organizer can confirm location features to admit missions and trials that require them. The server still checks whether two locations are available and whether the contact category is enabled. Actual contact requires agreement from the participants. Phones show the possessed situation and its capture and release conditions, or the exorcist mission and role objective, as short questions and answers. Possessed cards tell players to turn off their light after taking their place. The standalone editable bank remains separate from the new-party draw.

## Ordinary trials

The computer freezes seven eligible trials before the first deal. The affected player sees the personal instruction and the ordinary mission in the Missions dialog during exorcism. Witnesses provide their own answers without seeing the actor's or other witnesses' answers. In the two-witness description trial, the actor saves the three traits before the encounters, then names the two witnesses in a separate report. Witnesses can fill previously unanswered fields before the check is sealed. At checking the organizer can confirm equivalent wording for an individual T010 trait. The organizer also reviews any late T004 repeat visit separately before sealing. The hidden instruction and scoring conditions stay private. The branch is fixed from the number of opened prophecies when checking opens. An unavailable target or insufficient evidence earns no bonus and does not select a replacement. The trial can add at most one success and does not restore a captured exorcist's mission.

## Connection, display and saved state

The browser title and both player and organizer shells use the same Oderzhimye name. The laptop separates the roster, round controls and public ritual, with history available in settings after the party ends. The laptop and phones share a chronological How to Play guide. The phone places role, time and private card on one screen. Dependent-undo review shows the original event, blockers and server preview in its own dialog. The visible name is independent of the existing storage keys and catalog format.

The laptop shows initial roles and readiness. Current physical status is personal or appears during authorized recall/checking; other cards, the bank and unopened prophecies are never sent to the room. A phone can hide its card while keeping its phase and deadline. Disconnected timers are unknown. The laptop can skip candle playback; animation never changes saved state.

The executable saves the party in private data/party-v7.json. A valid locked version-six party opens in First Game without changing its original file, cards or settled outcomes. Locked checkpoints keep their mode, story, trials and exact issued card text, including any historical variant. Recovery discards the retired horror setting. Future authored draws use base cards plus any enabled comedy missions. An unlocked checkpoint adopts the new setup and keeps any previously saved story. On older installs the editable bank can be recovered from data/session.json without restoring or replacing that old party. Restart restores active placement/exorcism paused and keeps untimed recall or checking at the same step with its reports and decisions. On the same origin, phones keep their own cached view and IndexedDB journal. LAN HTTP supports the open tab; production HTTPS or localhost can install the offline app shell.

See [first-game design](../design/first-game.md) for ownership, contracts and acceptance. The author bank is not an original-deck transcription. Exact original card fidelity, real-device acceptance and physical balance remain separate work.
