# Current implementation

New rooms play **Ordinary** in [Oderzhimye with a computer Master](README.md). The organizer participates from a phone. The laptop admits five to twelve humans, hosts one room, shows the chosen prologue, gives phase calls, and displays the shared ritual. A party lasts up to seven rounds. A restored First Game party keeps its original mode.

## Prepare, deal and play

- Create a room and invite phones with the code or a LAN/Tuna link. The code in the toolbar opens the QR invitation throughout the party. The game chooses one story at room creation and saves it with the party. The organizer and shared display can read it from the Story button.
- Save the base, at least one permitted location and optional mission categories in Party Settings. Saving is available before players join. Preparation locks on the first deal, including after cancellation.
- Party Settings defaults to automatic door progress. It previews the threshold for the current roster once five players have joined. The organizer can enter a whole number from 6 to 84 steps or use Reset to Auto, then save. The threshold setting locks with preparation.
- Admission normally closes at the first deal. If fewer than five players remain before the first round, the organizer can cancel the deal and invite replacements up to five using the same room code. Roles are randomized among recipients with one extra exorcist for an odd roster. At later deals only people confirmed at the base get cards. A sole returning medium is an exorcist. Reading is untimed. The organizer sees readiness and pending replacements, can replace one card in place, and starts explicitly. Starting with unread or disconnected phones requires a warning. Pending replacements or an invalid composition block start.
- The laptop starts the placement timer, which defaults to 45 seconds. Newly possessed report a permitted location on their phone. Retained groups stay in place. A timed exorcism follows, with a default of four minutes. Exorcists explore individually. Releasing the last group leaves the exorcism timer running so free exorcists can finish their missions. Recall starts at the deadline, on the laptop's early call, when no active exorcist remains, or when the last active group loses its remaining members through departure. At ordinary recall only free people return; the laptop opens untimed checking separately.
- Possessed and captured players follow the same private group card. Possessed and captured players record their own infections and can correct them individually. The exorcist who met a release condition chooses a group from a list with its members' names. The server releases the entire group. The same dialog shows whom that exorcist released in the current round and offers available corrections. Group assignments, membership and reported places can survive a round boundary, and so can a group whose original holder leaves. Capturing and later releasing an exorcist never restores credit for that assignment's optional mission.

## Check and finish

An eligible exorcist confirms their own completion on the phone using the Mission Completed checkbox. Selecting it again opens a confirmation to clear the mark.

Phones upload durable capture, release, mission and correction reports. A player can request review when dependencies block an undo. The organizer can resolve blocking records before approving it. Online player actions apply immediately in eligible phases. An offline mission report received after recall requires an organizer decision. The organizer can preview both decisions with their score and dependency changes. Already recorded events can upload on pause without changing the result; new gameplay reports and undo are disabled on pause. Missing group places can be confirmed during checking and carry forward without retroactively changing releases. Connected players receive automatic base or group confirmation from the accepted ledger. The organizer confirms offline returns and corrects offline infection or release in the same roster. A confirmed infection with an unknown group cancels mission credit and delays dealing until release. Connection loss alone does not establish infection or return. Unconfirmed free people miss the next deal and can be confirmed at a subsequent check. The organizer can explicitly close waiting for missing journals; closed results are immutable.

The automatic Ordinary threshold is six steps per group of possessed at the start of the first round. Seven players start with 4/2/2 candles and need 18 steps. A manual threshold sets the exact number of steps. First Game and Ordinary rituals initialized before this change keep the original N+1/2/3 setup. Each confirmed rescue of an original assignment or eligible mission moves one candle. Players confirmed at the base can report a violation of an open prophecy on the phone during checking or while reading the next deal. The laptop reviews it and records the outcome once. Prophecies open in order and stay open after a backward step. A penalty selects the unique largest pile including candles off the board; if it is A or there is a tie, close the report without a move. The endings are victory, seventh-check defeat, confirmed total possession, no return after the configurable recall wait, and stop. A disputed release can delay the no-return decision. In every ending everyone returns.

## Authored cards

The authored bank contains 75 possessed missions, 42 exorcist missions, 10 ordinary prophecies, the final door, six stories and two references. Indoor, contact, harder and comedy missions start enabled in a new room. The outdoor subset starts disabled. The organizer can adjust the selection before the first deal. The optional comedy setting adds 24 possessed and 16 exorcist missions to the authored draw alongside the base missions. The server checks whether two locations are available and whether the contact category is enabled. New deals exclude missions that require other specific venue features, such as a mirror or a tree. Actual contact requires agreement from the participants. Phones show the possessed situation and its capture and release conditions, or the exorcist mission and role objective, as short questions and answers. Possessed cards tell players to turn off their light after taking their place. The authored exorcist missions require a short exchange or a scene another player sees or hears. Players record their own completion. The standalone editable bank remains separate from the new-party draw.

The organizer can confirm a new party during play or after the finale. The room code, invitation and participants carry over, including the resume keys for disconnected phones. The game returns to first-round preparation with the saved timings, venue, card selection and ritual target available for editing. Progress and issued cards are cleared and a new story is drawn. The organizer starts the first deal manually.

Stories, personal missions and ordinary prophecies keep their draw history across new parties and server restarts. The game prefers unseen eligible cards and starts a new cycle when those run out.

## Connection, display and saved state

The browser title and both player and organizer shells use the same Oderzhimye name. The laptop separates the roster, round controls and public ritual, with history available in settings after the party ends. The laptop and phones share a chronological How to Play guide. The phone places role, time and private card on one screen. Dependent-undo review shows the original event, blockers and server preview in its own dialog. The visible name is independent of the existing storage keys and catalog format.

The laptop shows initial roles and readiness. Current physical status is personal or appears during authorized recall/checking; other cards, the bank and unopened prophecies are never sent to the room. A phone can hide its card while keeping its phase and deadline. Disconnected timers are unknown. The laptop can skip candle playback; animation never changes saved state.

The executable saves the party in private data/party-v8.json. Version-seven and version-six saves migrate without changing the original files or settled outcomes. Locked checkpoints keep their mode, story and issued card text, including historical variants. Retired Dwelling missions are replaced during recovery while their assignment identities, accepted reports and results survive. The old venue field is discarded. Recovery discards the retired horror setting. Future authored draws use base cards plus any enabled comedy missions. An unlocked checkpoint adopts the new setup and keeps any previously saved story. On older installs the editable bank can be recovered from data/session.json without restoring or replacing that old party. Restart restores active placement/exorcism paused and keeps untimed recall or checking at the same step with its reports and decisions. On the same origin, phones keep their own cached view and IndexedDB journal. LAN HTTP supports the open tab; production HTTPS or localhost can install the offline app shell.

See [first-game design](../design/first-game.md) for ownership, contracts and acceptance. The author bank is not an original-deck transcription. Exact original card fidelity, real-device acceptance and physical balance remain separate work.
