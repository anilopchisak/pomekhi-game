# Current implementation

The executable defaults to **First Game** and also offers **Ordinary** in [Oderzhimye with a computer Master](README.md). The organizer participates from a phone. The laptop admits five to twelve humans, hosts one room, delivers the prologue and phase calls, and shows the common ritual. Both modes last up to seven rounds. First Game suppresses all trial instructions and scoring. Neither mode has a practice round or postgame continuation.

## Prepare, deal and play

- Create a room, invite phones with its code or the LAN/Tuna link and QR, and save the base, permitted locations, empty Spirit Dwelling, story and virtual table order. Read the selected prologue before issuing cards. Preparation locks on the first deal and stays locked if that deal is canceled. Select the indoor, outdoor, contact and harder subsets independently and confirm venue features. The shared Dwelling location is used by private offering missions. A report counts only toward the assigned mission.
- Admission normally closes at the first deal. If fewer than five players remain before the first round, the organizer can cancel the deal and invite replacements up to five using the same room code. New assignments alternate exorcist and possessed in table order. At later deals only people confirmed at the base get cards. A sole returning medium is an exorcist. Reading is untimed; readiness does not start the round. The laptop can replace one card without changing its role or other assignments.
- The laptop starts a 45-second placement deadline. Newly possessed report a permitted location on their phone. Retained groups stay in place. A four-minute exorcism follows; exorcists explore individually. The server can end exorcism at the deadline, when a side can no longer act, or on the laptop's early call. At ordinary recall only free people return; the laptop opens untimed checking separately.
- Possessed and captured players follow the same private group card. Physical actions are participant reports. Group assignments, membership and reported places can survive a round boundary, and so can a group whose original holder leaves. Capturing and later releasing an exorcist never restores credit for that assignment's optional mission.

## Check and finish

Phones upload durable capture, release, mission and correction reports. A player can request review when dependencies block an undo. The organizer can resolve blocking records before approving it. A new mission report arriving after recall always needs an explicit decision, including when the phone uses the direct reporting path. The organizer can preview both decisions with their score and dependency changes. Already recorded events can upload on pause without changing the result; new gameplay reports and undo are disabled on pause. Missing group places can be confirmed during checking and carry forward without retroactively changing releases. Physical status is a separate confirmation from a participant's phone or the laptop; connection loss is not evidence of captivity or return. Unconfirmed free people miss the next deal and can be confirmed at a subsequent check. The organizer can explicitly close waiting for missing journals; closed results are immutable.

The original candle formula is N+1 / 2 / 3. Each confirmed rescue of an original assignment or eligible mission moves one candle. Players confirmed at the base can report a violation of an open prophecy on the phone during checking or while reading the next deal. The laptop reviews it and records the outcome once. Prophecies open in order and stay open after a backward step. A penalty selects the unique largest pile including candles off the board; if it is A or there is a tie, close the report without a move. The four endings are victory, seventh-check defeat, confirmed total possession, and stop. In all four endings everyone returns; a new party has a new room identity.

## Authored cards

The default authored bank contains 75 possessed missions, 42 exorcist missions, 11 trials, 10 ordinary prophecies, the final door, six stories and two references. The host can mix in 38 horror variants. Each eligible variant replaces its corresponding mission; any eligible base mission without a replacement remains in the draw. Confirming the harder subset is a separate choice. Phones show the assigned card's behavior, capture, release and any venue-specific setup, or the exorcist mission and completion criterion. The authored cards use no prepared props. For the angel card, confirm an existing mirror and space to move in the same allowed room. First Game also offers the separate editable bank.

## Ordinary trials

The computer freezes seven eligible trials before the first deal. The affected player sees a personal instruction at exorcism. Witnesses provide their own answers without seeing the actor's or other witnesses' answers. In the two-witness description trial, the actor saves the three traits before the encounters, then names the two witnesses in a separate report. Witnesses can fill previously unanswered fields before the check is sealed. At checking the organizer can confirm equivalent wording for an individual T010 trait. The organizer also reviews any late T004 repeat visit separately before sealing. The hidden instruction and scoring conditions stay private. The branch is fixed from the number of opened prophecies when checking opens. An unavailable target or insufficient evidence earns no bonus and does not select a replacement. The trial can add at most one success and does not restore a captured exorcist's mission.

## Connection, display and saved state

The browser title and both player and organizer shells use the same Oderzhimye name. The laptop separates the roster, round controls and public ritual, with history available in settings after the party ends. The phone places role, time and private card on one screen. Dependent-undo review shows the original event, blockers and server preview in its own dialog. The visible name is independent of the existing storage keys and catalog format.

The laptop shows initial roles and readiness. Current physical status is personal or appears during authorized recall/checking; other cards, the bank and unopened prophecies are never sent to the room. A phone can hide its card while keeping its phase and deadline. Disconnected timers are unknown. The laptop can skip candle playback; animation never changes saved state.

The executable saves the party in private data/party-v7.json. A valid version-six party opens in First Game without changing its original file, cards or settled outcomes. On older installs the editable bank can be recovered from data/session.json without restoring or replacing that old party. Restart restores active placement/exorcism paused and keeps untimed recall or checking at the same step with its reports and decisions. On the same origin, phones keep their own cached view and IndexedDB journal. LAN HTTP supports the open tab; production HTTPS or localhost can install the offline app shell.

See [first-game design](../design/first-game.md) for ownership, contracts and acceptance. The author bank is not an original-deck transcription. Exact original card fidelity, real-device acceptance and physical balance remain separate work.
