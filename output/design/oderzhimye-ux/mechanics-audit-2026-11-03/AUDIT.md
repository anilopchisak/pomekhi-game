# Game mechanics audit of the current Figma prototype

The current mockup does not cover every accepted game mechanic completely. The basic loop and many exception screens exist, but several scenarios stop at placeholders or contradict the rules during navigation. This is a review of design coverage, not an audit of the running application.

## Scope and evidence

- File: https://www.figma.com/design/uv4SdpjWGNJORTyrInJa88.
- Authority: the current [target rules](../../../../docs/product/README.md), including five to twelve human players, a computer Master, seven rounds, and the paper-free/prop-free adaptation. The frozen source and hash are in target-rules-snapshot.md and audit-baseline.json.
- The live Safari/native Plugin API read captured all 404 registered roots, including responsive variants, dialogs, motion scenes and review catalogs. No registered roots were missing and no unregistered frame roots were found. These are not 404 different mechanics.
- live-screens.json preserves current text, action IDs and native reactions. focused-evidence.json and scene-edges.json reduce the evidence to reviewed scenarios and scene transitions.
- Figma MCP remained quota-limited; the existing Safari plugin runner was used for a read-only extraction. No design nodes or comments were changed. Only audit artifacts and browser-check logs were written locally.
- The current target rules now also exclude a vessel or other prepared prop at the Dwelling; this supersedes the earlier bowl-based adaptation. The audit uses the latest owning rules rather than the stale construction scripts.

## Findings in priority order

### F01 - Preserve phase when concealing a card (P1)

The 320px route P08W320 -> conceal -> P53W320 -> open -> P01W320 enters active exorcism from untimed reading, without organizer start or readiness. Mission and infection actions become available. Placement has the analogous P14W320 -> P40W320 -> P02W320 route. This violates the rule that temporary card access cannot change phase, role or permitted actions. Keep hidden/open specimens for reading, placement, active play and pause, or use one phase-preserving prototype model.

Evidence: native reactions, asserted Safari transitions, safari-reading-phase-leak.png. Target rules: lines 51, 59-61 and 136 in the frozen snapshot.

### F02 - Complete repeated rounds and recall after resume (P1)

L08 -> L42 -> L09 represents the first round with 45s placement and 240s exorcism. However, L42 -> pause L20 -> resume L01 loses the recall transition: L01 has no phase-expiry reaction. The second active round L45 and sole-rescuer round L46 also have no expiry/check route. Phone P13/P14 advance after 3s while showing a 45s placement clock. This is more than a lack of synchronized clocks: the prototype cannot carry its repeated-round flow to checking. Keep representative timings explicitly labeled if accelerated, and connect subsequent rounds, resume and seventh-check outcomes.

Evidence: exact root reactions in live-screens.json and focused-evidence.json. The 240s waiting path was not timed again in this audit. Target rules: lines 51, 73-75 and 112.

### F03 - Preserve ritual state through next-deal and final-history navigation (P1)

A10 displays 1/14/3 candles, 34 remaining steps, round four and an already-open first prophecy. Its next-deal action opens L14 with 9/6/3, 42 steps, round two and closed prophecies. Safari reproduced this exact jump. Similarly, the single L39 history specimen reached from victory, defeat and stop shows the initial 13/2/3 and 46-step state rather than the terminal state. Add state-consistent continuation/history specimens or parameterize the prototype; reopening information must not reset ritual progress or outcome.

Evidence: A10 -> L14 asserted in Safari, safari-penalty-reset.png; native L16/L17/L19 -> L39 links. Target rules: lines 47, 90, 104-106 and 116.

### F04 - Design an actual ordinary-mode Master trial (P1)

P42 describes where a private instruction would appear but contains no instruction. P43 has a report button without a card-specific question or outcome selection. L28 presents a generic trial success; its next action leads to the first-game L12 specimen with trial effects disabled. These states are entered only from review catalogs. No seven-trial sequence, zero/one-versus-two-open-prophecy outcome, unavailable target or individually documented actor-dependent adaptation is shown. The first-game no-effects explanation does not cover ordinary-mode trials. At least one complete source-reviewed trial scenario, both outcome branches and the private-report/reconciliation path are needed before claiming this mechanic is designed.

Evidence: P42, P43, L28, M02 and scene-edges.json. Target rules: lines 44, 59, 84 and 122-132.

### F05 - Replace the physical Dwelling instructions with the accepted prop-free version (P1)

L05 still tells the group to place a vessel remotely and put three random missions into it. The current target requires an agreed empty place, no placed vessel or marker, three private digital mission records and an on-site gesture/phrase while retaining the phone. M13 contains only a generic physical-contribution warning. There is no complete offering assignment/report specimen, nor the adapted visit/early-return/return-visit trial scenario. Rewrite preparation and add the corresponding private instructions and reports without inventing extra rewards or remote presence detection.

Evidence: L05, M13 and the absence of offering content in the full visible screen corpus. Target rules: lines 15-17, 29 and 138.

### F06 - Add the input side of prophecy penalties (P2)

A10B/A10 demonstrate a backward step after a supposedly confirmed penalty. No game screen provides a violation report, active-condition selection, review or confirmation that leads to A10B. The only entry is the review catalog. Add this real user path, including correction and the effect on the immutable ritual result. Keep equal-largest-pile and A-largest cases explicitly unresolved until the original ruling is available; do not invent a tie algorithm.

Evidence: full reaction inventory and all visible penalty/violation text. Target rules: line 106 and the remaining-source-interpretation section.

### F07 - Make revealed conditions available on active phones (P2)

The shared-screen first/two-open overviews and individual phone condition dialogs exist. Active phone help nevertheless opens M44P, where every prophecy is closed. M01PA has no ritual entry. The only phone overview with revealed conditions is the all-open victory branch M44VP. Add one-open/two-open active phone overviews and links to the already-created condition dialogs. This is a navigation/state gap, not missing detail-dialog artwork.

Evidence: M03P -> M44P; M01PA; M44P/M44VP; M40P/M41P. Target rules: lines 104 and 136.

### F08 - Complete preparation controls and story selection (P2)

The L06 story button has no native reaction and M34 is a placeholder entered only from review navigation. M23 renders category values as text without selection/apply/change states. M02 explains first-game play but does not offer the ordinary-mode selection. L04 mentions reordering without a demonstrated reorder/redeal state. Add concrete controls and their changed, locked and confirmation states; complete a story selection/prologue route after the source text is approved.

Evidence: L06/M34/M23/M02/L04 native text and reactions. The attempted Safari story check could not target the unregistered story action; its inertness is established from the native reaction inventory, not a claimed successful browser click. Target rules: lines 37, 45, 55 and 140-144.

### F09 - Cover the missing physical-play instructions and early recall (P2)

The cards explain solo exploration, staying in place and returning after release. They do not instruct possessed/infected players to turn off their light. The ordinary flashlight restriction is absent; dim-screen copy alone is incomplete. No participant report for coalitions/excessive conversation or early round recall is shown; the available whole-party stop ends the party and is not the same operation. Add role/phase-specific lighting instructions, a report-driven early ritual call and clear card/prophecy precedence with technical-help exceptions.

Evidence: corpus matches and L01/L42 action inventory. Target rules: lines 31, 61, 65-75 and 108.

### F10 - Finish correction and eligibility scenarios (P2)

The connected undo example covers a mission report only. Infection and group-release reports have confirmation/feedback but no comparable own-report correction path. P45 explains a blocked dependent undo but has no ordinary-flow entry; P44 explains lost mission eligibility after infection but is also a standalone specimen. Demonstrate mission-recorded -> infection -> release -> no mission credit, plus safe correction and dependent-event review for infection/release. Physical rescues and group membership cannot be inferred from missing phone responses.

Evidence: P20/M28, M11/P17, M12/P54, P44/P45 and their inbound edges. Target rules: lines 83-88 and the preserved technical requirements.

### F11 - Distinguish ending artwork from a complete ending scenario (P2)

Victory, seventh-round defeat, total-possession defeat, already-free-at-table exception and explicit stop are drawn. Several are entered only through the review catalog, and the repeated-round route cannot currently reach them. The final result/history resets to a generic initial ritual specimen as noted in F03. Connect the decisive check, terminal announcement, all-player recall and retained outcome without permitting another scored round. There is no need to draw seven identical rounds; one consistent repeated-round model and explicit terminal branches are sufficient.

Evidence: L16-L19, L38-L39 and reachability.json. Target rules: lines 112-116.

### F12 - Keep the content dependency explicit

cards-reference.json contains 90 authored Pomekhi proposals with original_deck_verified=false. Full mission specimens p03/e06, three authored prophecy examples and generic story/trial placeholders do not establish a complete original deck. Card-dependent prop-free adaptations, mirror-specific releases, stories and trial variants need source-by-source review. This is a known content dependency, not a request to invent missing original text.

Evidence: ../cards-reference.json; M18/M31/M34/M40/P42. Target rules: lines 17-19, 35 and 132-138.
